## Description
DungeonTamheomga ("Dungeon Explorer") is a turn-based roguelike where every step is a turn.
Descend floor by floor through a dungeon that is generated anew on every run, fight monsters, pick up gear, and defeat the boss that waits on every fifth floor.

Start as a Warrior, a Rogue, or a Mage. The Warrior cuts down everything around him with Whirlwind, the Rogue hides in the shadows and strikes for triple damage, and the Mage fires magic missiles that ignore armor.

The dungeon holds shops, treasure vaults, altars that bless or curse, and traps you cannot see until it is too late. Boss slams are telegraphed with a red area one turn ahead: read it, step out, and you live.

Falling is not the end. Each run earns soul stones, and soul stones buy permanent upgrades that make the next attempt a little easier.

The final boss, the Void Lord, waits on floor 50. Beyond it the dungeon goes on forever. How deep can you go?

- No account and no internet connection required. Records are stored only on this PC.
- Available in English and Korean.
- A small game whose art, sound effects, and music are all generated by code.

## Features (one per line)

- Common, rare, and legendary gear with special properties
- Shops, treasure vaults, altars, and traps
- Soul stones and nine permanent upgrades that persist after death
