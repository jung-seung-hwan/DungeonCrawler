## Short description

## Description
One step, one turn. Nothing moves until you do.

DungeonTamheomga ("Dungeon Explorer") is a turn-based roguelike that never rushes you and never forgives a careless step. The dungeon is rebuilt every time you enter, and when you fall you start again from floor 1. No two runs are the same.

■ Three classes, three ways to fight
· Warrior — being surrounded is your opening. One Whirlwind cuts down everything around you.
· Rogue — vanish into the shadows, then land a single ambush for triple damage.
· Mage — finish fights from a distance with magic missiles that ignore armor.

■ Choices on every floor
Spend your gold at the shop or save it? Touch the altar that might bless you or curse you? The treasure vault is tempting, but the floor hides traps you cannot see until you step on them. With some luck, one legendary item turns the whole run around.

■ A boss every five floors
Boss slams are marked in red one turn before they land. Step out and you live; get greedy for one more hit and you don't. On floor 50 the final boss, the Void Lord, is waiting.

■ What you keep when you die
Every fall earns soul stones. Spend them on nine permanent upgrades and your next run starts a little stronger, including Revive, which lets you get back up once.

■ There is no bottom
Beat the Void Lord and the dungeon keeps going down. Your best floor per class and your top ten runs are kept on record. How deep can you go?

· Offline single player: no account, no internet, and your records stay on this PC.
· Keyboard and mouse, English and Korean
· 42 sound effects and 4 music tracks
